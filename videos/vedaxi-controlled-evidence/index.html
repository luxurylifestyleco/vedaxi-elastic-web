Cognitive Attention Recovery and Sensor Calibration in Analytical Workflows

Methodology walkthrough · recorded session · runtime 00:03:30

Method 1

Sensor apparatus

  • Eye tracking Dual infrared trackers at 250 Hz, nine point calibration at session start, revalidated at midpoint
  • EEG 32 channels, referenced to linked mastoids, continuous through the full task block
  • Synchronisation Both streams timestamped against a shared hardware clock, aligning pupil events to task boundaries within one sampling window

Method 2

Recording conditions and known drift

  • Ambient light Indirect daylight; afternoon sessions showed a baseline pupil diameter shift unrelated to task demand
  • Movement Posture change displaced the head position estimate beyond original calibration tolerance
  • Handling Conditions logged as they occurred rather than corrected in software, so retention decisions stay auditable after collection

Method 3

Pre-modeling data sanitization

  • Criterion 1 Calibration validity confirmed at both checkpoints
  • Criterion 2 Continuous signal coverage across the full task block
  • Criterion 3 Agreement between eye tracking and EEG timestamps
  • Order of work Exclusion list fixed before any outcome variable was inspected
